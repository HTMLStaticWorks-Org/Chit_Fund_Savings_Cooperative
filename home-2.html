Understanding Mutual Mechanics

How Savings Discipline & Periodic Fund Access Coexist

A cooperative chit scheme serves two complementary member priorities within a single group: members who wish to accumulate disciplined monthly savings toward the later stages of the cycle, and members who require planned access to a lump sum earlier in the tenure.

For Non-Prized Subscribers (Steady Savers)

While you remain non-prized, you contribute your monthly subscription reduced by your equal share of the monthly auction dividend. In the later months of the cycle, competition typically moderates, allowing patient subscribers to bid at lower discounts.

For Prized Subscribers (Planned Fund Recipients)

When you have a planned household, education, or business requirement, you may participate in the monthly auction within the bylaw discount ceiling. Upon being declared the prized subscriber and furnishing required sureties, you receive the net prize payout while continuing your monthly payments until scheme closure.

Sample Mathematical Illustration (30-Member ₹3,00,000 Group)

If the winning bid in Month 4 offers a sample discount of ₹45,000 (15%): the 5% foreman commission (₹15,000) is retained for group administration, the prized subscriber receives ₹2,55,000 upon surety verification, and the remaining ₹30,000 dividend is divided equally among all 30 members (₹1,000 credit per member).

Lifecycle of a Chit Group

Member Participation Timeline

Every subscriber moves through five documented milestones from initial eligibility review to final settlement.

Stage 1

Pre-Enrolment
Counseling

Review available scheme tiers, verify that the gross monthly instalment fits comfortably within your recurring income, and inspect all fee clauses.

Stage 2

KYC & Bylaws
Execution

Submit identity, residence, and income proofs along with nominee particulars. Sign the formal Chit Agreement and receive your member passbook.

Stage 3

Monthly Subscription
& Dividend

Remit monthly instalments before the due date. Receive official payment receipts and monthly ledger statements showing the auction dividend credit.

Stage 4

Auction Bidding &
Surety Verification

Participate in the monthly tender or draw. When declared a prized subscriber, submit guarantor/security documents to receive the net prize payout via bank transfer.

Stage 5

Scheme Closure &
Lien Release

Upon completion of the final monthly cycle and full reconciliation of group accounts, all surety obligations are discharged with a formal closure certificate.

Institutional Safeguards

Transparency & Trust Highlights

Our cooperative template emphasizes operational discipline, verifiable record-keeping, and clear member rights.

Safeguard 01 • Ledger Access

Written Auction Minutes &
Passbook Entries

Every monthly auction outcome—including the winning ticket number, bid discount amount, foreman commission deducted, and per-member dividend—is recorded in the group minutes book for member inspection.

Safeguard 02 • Banking Discipline

Traceable Non-Cash
Prize Disbursals

All prize payouts are disbursed strictly through crossed account-payee cheques or verified NEFT/RTGS bank transfers into the prized subscriber's registered bank account after surety verification.

Safeguard 03 • Fee Predictability

Capped Foreman Commission
& Documented Rules

Administrative charges are fixed in the scheme agreement prior to commencement. No unannounced deductions are levied during prize settlement.

Safeguard 04 • Member Recourse

Structured Two-Tier
Grievance Redressal

Members have direct access to the Branch Grievance Officer and the Cooperative Supervisory Committee for timely resolution of any ledger, receipt, or surety query.
