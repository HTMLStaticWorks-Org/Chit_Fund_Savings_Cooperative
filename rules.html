Clause 01

Membership Eligibility

Participation in any chit scheme organized by the cooperative is open strictly to individuals and eligible entities who satisfy the cooperative's admission criteria:

  • Applicants must be at least 18 years of age, of sound legal capacity, and residing or operating a verifiable trade within the cooperative's operational area.
  • The applicant's recurring monthly disposable income or business cash flow must comfortably cover the gross monthly instalment of the chosen scheme tier.
  • Admission to a specific chit group is subject to verification of identity, address, banking track record, and approval by the Foreman / Enrolment Committee.
Clause 02

Registration and Documentation

Prior to ticket allotment in any chit group, every prospective subscriber must complete formal documentation:

  • Submission of the Membership Application Form along with self-attested copies of official Government Photo ID and current Residence Proof.
  • Proof of income or financial standing (latest salary slips, 6-to-12-month bank statement, or business ITR/registration as applicable to the scheme tier).
  • Execution of the duplicate Chit Agreement acknowledging the gross value, instalment count, foreman commission, auction schedule, and surety conditions.
  • Payment of the one-time sample documentation and passbook issuance charge as specified in the scheme schedule.
Clause 03

Contribution and Instalment Deadlines

Timely remittance of monthly subscriptions is the foundation of a cooperative chit pool:

  • Monthly instalments fall due on the date printed in the scheme passbook, typically at least three (3) working days prior to the scheduled monthly auction.
  • Subscriptions may be paid via crossed cheque, demand draft, NEFT/RTGS/UPI transfer to the designated cooperative group collection account, or at the branch counter against an official numbered receipt.
  • Only non-prized subscribers whose instalments are paid in full up to the current cycle are eligible to participate in that month's auction or draw.
Clause 04

Late Payment Rules and Applicable Charges

To protect punctual members and ensure timely prize disbursal, overdue subscriptions are governed by clear sample rules:

  • A grace window of seven (7) calendar days from the published due date is permitted for non-prized members (though auction participation requires payment before the auction hour).
  • Payments received after the grace window attract a flat administrative notice charge (sample ₹50 to ₹400 depending on tier) plus overdue interest at the sample template rate (1.0% to 1.5% per month) as stipulated in the registered agreement.
  • Persistent delay by a non-prized subscriber across consecutive months may result in forfeiture of dividend eligibility for the delayed cycle.
Clause 05

Auction or Draw Procedures

Each monthly cycle features a transparent procedure to determine the prized subscriber:

  • Auctions are conducted on the fixed monthly date and time at the branch hall (or recorded hybrid session) in the presence of participating subscribers.
  • Eligible non-prized members bid a discount percentage on the gross chit value, starting from the 5% minimum floor (Foreman Commission) up to the scheme's maximum ceiling (e.g., 25%, 30%, or 35% sample cap).
  • The member offering the highest discount within the permitted ceiling is declared the Prized Subscriber for that month.
  • Where two or more eligible subscribers offer the identical maximum ceiling discount, the Prized Subscriber is selected by an open, witnessed lot draw among those tied bidders.
Clause 06

Prize Amount and Applicable Deductions

Calculation of the net prize payout and member dividend follows a transparent formula:

  • Prize Amount Formula: Gross Chit Value minus the Winning Bid Discount.
  • Foreman Commission Deduction: 5% of the Gross Chit Value (sample template rate) is retained by the Foreman toward group management, accounting, and statutory administration.
  • Dividend Distribution: The balance of the winning discount (Winning Bid Discount minus 5% Foreman Commission) is divided equally among all subscribers in the group and credited to reduce their next month's payable subscription.
  • Applicable statutory verification or stamp/documentation charges for surety execution are borne by the prized subscriber at actuals.
Clause 07

Member Responsibilities

Every subscriber entering a group assumes reciprocal obligations toward fellow members:

  • Both non-prized and prized subscribers must remit every monthly instalment until the final termination month of the group.
  • Upon winning an auction or draw, the prized subscriber must furnish acceptable sureties (guarantors or collateral/deposit security covering future unpaid instalments) within the stipulated verification window prior to disbursement.
  • Members must promptly notify the branch of any change in residential address, bank account, mobile number, or guarantor status.
Clause 08

Foreman Responsibilities

The Foreman serves as the administrative fiduciary for each chit group:

  • Maintaining complete, accurate subscriber registers, passbooks, receipt books, and monthly auction minutes.
  • Conducting auctions punctually on the announced schedule and circulating dividend notices to all subscribers.
  • Verifying sureties fairly and disbursing the net prize amount via traceable bank transfer within the timeframe prescribed in the scheme agreement once sureties are approved.
  • Replacing defaulting non-prized subscribers in accordance with the bylaws so that group liquidity remains uninterrupted.
Clause 09

Default and Non-Payment Procedures

Clear procedures govern defaults by either non-prized or prized subscribers:

  • Non-Prized Default: If a non-prized subscriber fails to remit instalments for three (3) consecutive months despite written notice, the Foreman may remove the defaulting subscriber from the register and induct a substitute member. The net actual amount contributed by the removed subscriber (less the 5% foreman fee and documented damages) is refunded upon substitution or at scheme termination per the registered agreement.
  • Prized Subscriber Default: If a prized subscriber defaults after receiving the prize payout, all remaining future instalments become recoverable from the subscriber and their co-signing sureties/collateral after due written notice.
Clause 10

Nominee and Transfer Provisions

Provisions for continuity, assignment, or nomination of a member's ticket:

  • Every subscriber must designate a legal nominee at the time of enrolment. In the unfortunate event of a non-prized subscriber's demise, the nominee may either continue the ticket (subject to eligibility) or claim settlement per the bylaws.
  • A non-prized subscriber may request transfer of their ticket to an eligible third party only with the written consent of the Foreman and completion of full KYC by the transferee.
Clause 11

Scheme Completion and Settlement

Orderly closure of the chit group at the end of its scheduled tenure:

  • In the final month of the scheme, the last non-prized subscriber receives the gross chit value minus the standard 5% foreman commission.
  • Upon reconciliation of all subscriber ledgers, the cooperative issues a formal No-Dues & Scheme Completion Certificate and releases all guarantor bonds and security liens within 15 working days.
Clause 12

Complaints and Grievance Redressal

Accessible channels for addressing member concerns fairly and promptly:

  • Tier 1 — Branch Member Relations Desk: Any discrepancy regarding receipts, dividend calculation, or surety verification may be submitted in writing or via the online form for resolution within 5 working days.
  • Tier 2 — Cooperative Grievance & Audit Committee: If unresolved at Tier 1, members may escalate the matter to the Supervisory Grievance Officer for documented review within 15 working days.
  • Statutory Recourse: Where applicable under local law, disputes remaining unresolved are subject to the arbitration/registrar mechanism specified in the registered Chit Agreement.
