Complete Operational Clarity

Financial Transparency & Governance Framework

Understanding how funds are pooled, how auctions operate, and what obligations rest with both the Foreman and subscribers is essential before joining any chit group.

Read Full Rules & Bylaws
Pillar 01

Contribution Structure

A chit group consists of a fixed number of members equal to the number of months (e.g., 30 members for 30 months). Each member subscribes an equal gross periodic instalment, forming the monthly pot.

Pillar 02

Scheme Duration

Tenures run for a predetermined cycle (such as 25, 30, 40, or 50 months). Every non-prized subscriber is assured of receiving the prize pool once during the tenure, either by competitive bid or scheduled draw.

Pillar 03

Charges & Fees

In our sample template structure, the cooperative Foreman deducts a standard 5% commission from the gross chit value for group administration, plus a nominal one-time enrolment documentation charge.

Pillar 04

Auction or Draw Procedures

Each month, non-prized members in good standing may bid a discount (between the 5% floor and the scheme's maximum cap, e.g., 25%–30%). If multiple members bid the cap, a witnessed lot draw selects the prized subscriber.

Pillar 05

Foreman Responsibilities

The Foreman is responsible for enrolling verified subscribers, conducting monthly auctions on schedule, recording minutes, distributing dividends accurately, and disbursing prize money upon surety verification.

Pillar 06

Member Obligations

Both non-prized and prized subscribers are obligated to pay monthly instalments until the final month of the group. Prized subscribers must furnish acceptable sureties prior to receiving the prize payout.

Common Member Enquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding membership admission, monthly instalments, auction procedures, withdrawals, and scheme completion.

Prospective members begin by submitting an enquiry and attending a verification consultation. Standard admission requires government photo identification, proof of residence, recent income or bank statements demonstrating capacity to meet the monthly commitment, nominee details, and execution of the scheme agreement.
Each scheme has a fixed gross monthly instalment (for example, ₹10,000 in a ₹3,00,000 / 30-member group). However, when a member wins the monthly auction at a discount above the 5% foreman commission, the remaining discount is distributed equally among all members as a dividend—reducing the net payable subscription for that cycle. Because auction bids vary each month, net payable instalments fluctuate and are never guaranteed to be a fixed amount.
Auctions are held on the scheduled monthly date announced in the scheme calendar. Non-prized subscribers whose instalments are paid up to date may submit bids within the permitted discount floor (5%) and maximum cap (e.g., 25%–35%). The subscriber offering the highest discount within the cap is declared the prized subscriber. If multiple eligible members bid the maximum cap, the winner is selected via an open, documented lot draw.
Because a prized subscriber receives the lump-sum payout early in the cycle while still owing instalments for the remaining months, the cooperative requires verifiable security (such as personal guarantors with documented income or acceptable collateral/deposit liens) proportional to the remaining unpaid future liability before releasing the funds.
Chit schemes rely on every seat in the group remaining active. If a non-prized member requests withdrawal or defaults, they may either substitute an approved replacement member or await settlement of their actual net paid subscriptions (less applicable foreman commission and documented administrative deductions per the registered bylaws) upon scheme completion or replacement induction.
In the final month of the scheme, the last remaining non-prized subscriber receives the gross chit value less only the standard 5% foreman commission. Once all monthly subscriptions are reconciled, surety liens are formally released and a no-dues completion certificate is issued to every member.
