Dedicated to Structured Mutual Savings & Member Trust

Sahayog Financial Cooperative is designed around a straightforward premise: when financial rules, monthly subscription responsibilities, and auction deductions are explained transparently from day one, members can plan their household and business finances with confidence.

Who We Are (Editable Profile)

A Member-Focused Cooperative Built on Mutual Accountability

Rotating savings and credit groups—formally structured as chit schemes—have long served families, salaried professionals, and small business proprietors seeking disciplined monthly savings paired with periodic access to pooled capital.

Our service philosophy places suitability and education ahead of enrolment. Rather than encouraging prospective subscribers into high-value groups that strain monthly budgets, our coordinators review income stability, explain the obligation to continue payments after winning a prize, and ensure every participant understands the surety documentation required before payout.

Core Institutional Values

Our Mission & Guiding Principles

Every interaction at our cooperative is guided by six foundational commitments to our members.

01

Member-First
Suitability

We guide prospective members toward monthly instalment tiers that align prudently with their verified recurring household or enterprise cash flow.

02

Complete Fee
Clarity

Foreman commission rates, documentation fees, and overdue notice charges are stated explicitly in writing before any agreement is signed.

03

Equal Access
to Rules

Every subscriber—whether joining a ₹1,00,000 entry group or a ₹20,00,000 business group—receives the same documented bylaws and auction rights.

04

Disciplined Financial
Planning

We encourage consistent monthly saving habits that help members prepare for planned milestones such as education, shop inventory, or home improvements.

05

Responsible
Communication

We strictly avoid speculative claims, exaggerated wealth imagery, or promises of fixed investment returns. Mutual chit dividends vary by monthly auction bids.

06

Accessible Member
Support

Our branch officers maintain regular consultation hours to assist members with passbook reconciliation, nominee updates, and surety preparation.

Operational Standards

Transparency & Accountability in Practice

How our cooperative safeguards group integrity throughout the lifecycle of every chit scheme.

Standard 01

Pre-Admission KYC & Capacity Screening

Because each subscriber relies on fellow group members paying on time, we verify identity, residence, and payment capacity for every seat in the group prior to commencement.

Standard 02

Documented Monthly Auction Minutes

Every tender and draw session is recorded with participating member signatures, detailing the gross pot, winning discount, foreman fee, and per-ticket dividend.

Standard 03

Structured Surety & Lien Release

Surety verification protects non-prized members from default risk, while prompt lien discharge upon scheme completion respects the prized subscriber's security.
