Understanding Applicable Charges, Auctions & Eligibility

Every prospective subscriber should review how charges and monthly auctions operate prior to signing a scheme agreement.

01 • Applicable Charges & Fees

Foreman Commission & Documentation

Each sample scheme carries a 5% foreman commission on the gross chit value (deducted when disbursing the prize amount) and a nominal one-time enrolment documentation fee (₹250 to ₹1,000 sample). Late instalments beyond the 7-day grace window attract documented administrative notice fees.

02 • Auction & Draw Protocol

Competitive Bidding & Tie-Breaker Lots

Monthly auctions open at a 5% floor (covering foreman commission) up to the scheme's ceiling (25%–35%). The discount foregone above 5% is distributed equally as a dividend among all members. If multiple subscribers bid the ceiling, a witnessed lot draw determines the prized member.

03 • Participation & Surety Norms

KYC Admission & Future Liability Security

Enrolment requires valid photo ID, local residence proof, and income verification matching the monthly commitment. Prized subscribers must provide acceptable personal guarantors or deposit/collateral security covering remaining unpaid instalments before prize payout.
